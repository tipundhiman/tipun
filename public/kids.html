Information for parents and guardians

This limited Kids Studio is an on-device creative preview, no online child account. Under-13 cloud accounts and social features remain unavailable until age-appropriate safety controls and a verified parental-consent process are implemented and reviewed. Do not share identifying information in nicknames or lyrics.

To ask about future family accounts, email RhymeOrbit support from your own email. Please do not send a child's name, date of birth, photo or other identifiers.

The app and browser use the same RhymeOrbit website experience. Local drafts may not sync between devices. You can remove the profile and songbook above or clear browser/app storage.
