Safety standards

Protecting people on RhymeOrbit

Our community must be safe for creators. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, harassment, sexual solicitation of minors and other abuse.

Our rules

  • Never exploit, sexualize, threaten, groom or solicit a child.
  • Never create, upload, share or request child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated depictions.
  • Never pressure anyone to disclose private information, contact details, locations, images or recordings.
  • Users must only share media they have a right to upload. We may restrict accounts or remove content that violates these rules.

Report safety concerns

Use RhymeOrbit's in-app support or send a report to dtipun@gmail.com. Include an account handle, content link and a short description if safely available. Do not send or re-share illegal images or videos. We review reports, remove prohibited content when identified and cooperate with lawful requests as applicable. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Child-safety contact for Google Play and related inquiries: dtipun@gmail.com.

Age-appropriate access

For visitors under 13, the current Kids Studio is an on-device writing space with public social sharing, Vibe Match, chat, microphone, uploads and AI requests unavailable. It does not create an online child account. We will not enable online child profiles until parental-consent and other required protections are independently verified.

Our age-selection screen is a preliminary experience setting, not a verified identity check. We continue to review moderation, reporting, age gating, privacy and region-specific requirements. Account deletion requests are available separately.
