Privacy & account controls

Delete your RhymeOrbit account

You can request deletion of your account and associated RhymeOrbit data even if you have uninstalled the Android app. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Request account deletion

We verify ownership before deleting an account. If you still have access to the registered email, send the request from that email. If you have lost access, contact support and we will discuss alternative verification.

This form prepares a message in your email application. It does not transmit your details to RhymeOrbit until you send the email. Please do not send passwords, OTPs or identification documents in the initial request.

Email support directly
Your email application should open with the request ready. Please send the message to complete your request. If no email app opened, use the support address or copy the request text below.

What will be deleted?

  • Your account and profile details.
  • Associated RhymeOrbit projects, generated-song records, posts, messages and stored media managed by RhymeOrbit, subject to identity verification and processing.
  • Some data may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations; we will explain any such exceptions.

Downloaded files and browser-only drafts or local singing recordings on your device are under your control. Clearing browser/app storage removes those local copies. Records held by connected providers may require separate deletion requests.

What happens next?

RhymeOrbit support reviews the request, verifies the account owner, and coordinates deletion of associated service data. Sending a request is not an automatic deletion and may need follow-up. We aim to handle verified requests without unreasonable delay.
