Content & Copyright Policy

How to share authorized work and report suspected copyright or other content-rights infringement.

Updated 24 October 2026 · Contact: dtipun@gmail.com

Respect original creators

Only upload music, recordings, artwork, lyrics and other content you created or have permission to use. A sample pack, beat, AI output or Creative Commons track may carry attribution, commercial-use and other license conditions. Check the exact terms before publishing it on RhymeOrbit or elsewhere.

Report a rights concern

Email dtipun@gmail.com with the subject “RhymeOrbit Copyright Notice” and include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the work or right you own or represent.
  • The exact RhymeOrbit page URL or content identifier at issue.
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization, and a description of the alleged infringement.
  • A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized, and confirmation that your information is accurate.

Our response

Reports may be reviewed, the affected content may be restricted while a claim is evaluated, and relevant users may be contacted. Where applicable, content creators can respond or provide evidence that they have the required rights. Sending a report does not automatically guarantee removal or a particular response time.

AI-generated music

Using an AI tool does not automatically establish copyright ownership or clearance to imitate a specific artist, voice, song or recording. Avoid unauthorized voice clones, deceptive impersonation and copyrighted lyrics or samples. Provider permissions and applicable laws remain important.

Privacy and safety concerns

To report personal information, harassment or harmful posts, use the same support email with a link and relevant information. Do not include unnecessary private information about others.

Need assistance? Contact RhymeOrbit.
