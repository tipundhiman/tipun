NEW: Free Vocal Lab — record and clean your voice

Record your vocals or upload a short audio file, apply adjustable noise gating, vocal EQ and compression, compare the before-and-after sound, and export a WAV. Processing happens entirely in your browser: no account, GPU or audio upload required. This is basic audio cleanup, not AI denoising, autotune or voice cloning.

Try the free Vocal Lab ↗

NEW: Free 50-second animated Music Video Maker

Combine five original 10-second animated scenes, your own music and timed lyrics into one video. It works privately in supported desktop browsers, without GPU hosting or trial AI video APIs. The scenes are designed animations, not footage generated by a text-to-video model.
