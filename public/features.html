AI Songwriting & Creator Features

Explore RhymeOrbit's AI lyrics, Shayari, vocal song generation, creative projects, Community and Vibe Match.

Creator Lab — 20 free tools for creators worldwide

Draft multilingual hook prompts, check lyrics, build arrangements and synchronized LRC files, tap a tempo, run an audio metronome, transpose keys, record short private voice memos and plan global releases. These tools run in your browser; no song credits or paid API needed. English rhyme and syllable helpers are approximations and voice memo format depends on your browser.

Open Creator Lab ↗

Free Vocal Lab — record, clean and tune your voice

Record vocals, upload an audio file, or open a saved RhymeOrbit song directly from Download Audio. Adjust low-level noise gating, EQ and compression, and optionally test experimental solo-vocal pitch correction. Compare before/after sound and export a WAV privately in your browser. This is not neural noise separation or professional Auto-Tune.

Try the free Vocal Lab ↗

Sing Your Song — karaoke and free singing classes

Practise with original songs or search playable Audius community audio. Change, clear or restore original practice lyrics, or paste your authorized lyrics for Audius songs. Signed-in users can optionally ask Gemini to transcribe their own uploaded song or voice-only singing take into editable lines, after confirming rights and consent; the selected audio is sent for processing, while existing recordings remain local unless submitted. AI lyrics and timing require review and are not a licensed lyrics catalog for third-party tracks. The Sing Along microphone can reduce Audius playback volume while you sing; Audius recordings contain your voice, not the streamed music. LRC timestamps sync when supplied; plain lyrics use estimated timing. Pitch practice is educational, not a professional singing grade.

Open Sing Your Song ↗

Animated Music Video Maker and My Videos

Add your own song or generate lyrics, choose original animated characters and export repeating 10-second themed scenes for up to five minutes. Line-level timestamps let you sync captions with the actual song; AI-estimated timing still needs review. MP4 is used when supported with audio, otherwise WebM. New videos download to your device, while previously saved R2 videos remain accessible in the archive. No paid video-generation API is used; these are preset animations, not generative cinematic footage.

Use AI Studio to draft original song lyrics, experiment with genres and moods, and get feedback on song structure. Supported text generation requires signing in and depends on the Gemini API integration being configured.

Riya — your AI creative guide

Riya is an in-site chat assistant for writing inspiration and practical help using RhymeOrbit. Signed-in AI replies use the configured model service. Answers can be inaccurate.

Full songs with vocals — available for eligible users

RhymeOrbit uses a connected music provider to generate full songs with vocals and instrumentals. Eligible signed-in Free users can create up to 3 songs per calendar month, subject to provider availability and server limits. Paid plans and commercial-use rights are not automatically included; review the applicable terms.

Listen & Chill — music and video collections

Browse Audius songs by language, mood, real play count or release date, or explore its worldwide weekly trending list. YouTube videos use the official embedded player. When live YouTube Data API search is unavailable, clearly labelled featured videos appear instead of a fictional trending chart.
