Privacy Policy

How RhymeOrbit handles accounts, uploaded content, AI requests, storage and privacy requests.

Updated 25 October 2026 · Contact: dtipun@gmail.com

1. Data you provide

When you register, RhymeOrbit may collect your username, email, display name, first and last name, region, optional gender preference and account credentials handled by the authentication provider. Lyrics, saved project details, creative posts, media uploads, reports and messages you choose to submit may also be stored.

2. Why we process it

We use account information to sign you in and personalize the experience; project and media data to provide the features you request; abuse reports to operate moderation; and basic service and error data for security, support and performance.

3. AI prompts and generated content

When you use AI tools, the prompts, lyrics or chat text needed to fulfill the request are transmitted to the configured AI provider. In Sing Your Song, a song or microphone recording you explicitly choose to transcribe is sent to Google Gemini only after you confirm rights and consent. RhymeOrbit does not intentionally retain a copy of that transcription audio on its servers; Google's handling of submitted audio follows its own terms and privacy practices. AI-generated transcripts may contain errors. Do not send passwords, financial records, government identifiers or other sensitive information in prompts or recordings. Riya chat history is kept in the current browser page's memory for conversational context and is not intentionally saved to a separate RhymeOrbit chat database by the chat widget.

4. Third-party providers

RhymeOrbit integrates Supabase for account authentication and database services, Cloudflare R2 for supported media uploads, Gemini for text and opt-in audio AI, and a music generation provider when enabled. Audius, Jamendo, YouTube and Google Fonts may be contacted when their music, video or other features load. Search terms, device/network information and playback requests may be processed by these providers under their own policies, sometimes outside India.

5. Browser storage and singing

Browser storage may hold sessions, drafts, preferences, edited practice lyrics and local singing recordings. Sing Your Song saves local recordings in your browser until you delete them or clear browser data; downloadable copies remain under your control. Audius and Indie Sing Along record only your microphone, excluding their streamed backing tracks. YouTube videos are delivered by YouTube. Browser autosave is not a cloud backup. We have not intentionally added advertising tracking cookies; third-party embedded services may use their own technologies.

6. Visibility and ownership

Posts and media shared publicly in Community can be visible to others. Saved private projects are intended for your authenticated account. You are responsible for not uploading confidential content you are not authorized to share.

7. Retention and security

We keep data as needed for operating the service, legal obligations and security. Exact retention periods depend on the data type and external provider. No online service can promise absolute security; use a unique password and avoid sharing sensitive information.

8. Privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, email dtipun@gmail.com from your registered email and describe the request. Identity verification may be needed. Some records may be retained where legally required. We will provide instructions or an update through email.

9. Changes

This notice will be updated as providers, features or legal requirements change. Check this page for the latest version.
