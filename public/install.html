Your music universe, one tap away

Take your orbit with you.

Install the same RhymeOrbit experience on your Home Screen. Listen & Chill, Sing Your Song, AI Studio and Community stay connected to the website you already use.

Follow the steps below on your phone or computer.
01 / ANDROID

Android · Chrome

Open rhymeorbit.in in Chrome. Tap the browser menu ⋮, choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

02 / IPHONE

iPhone · Safari

Open RhymeOrbit in Safari. Tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. The RhymeOrbit icon appears with your apps.

03 / DESKTOP

Desktop browser

Open RhymeOrbit in Chrome or another PWA-capable browser. Use its Install action in the address bar or browser menu.

Free public beta. This is an installable website, not yet a Play Store or App Store release. Music playback, AI tools, login and cloud libraries require an internet connection. No private songs or account data are cached for offline playback.
