Turn your story into music

RhymeOrbit brings songwriting and music creation into one workflow. You can write from scratch, generate lyrics with AI, or paste original verses and choruses. The song generator supports a vocal track alongside the instrumental arrangement when the music service is available.

01 / WRITE

Ideas or full lyrics

Describe the feeling you want, or bring your own lyrics. Use clear verse and chorus sections when you have a complete song.

02 / CHOOSE

Voice and style

Choose auto, male, female or duet voice requests. Guide the sound with a genre, mood or simple style description.

03 / CREATE

Listen and save

Follow progress in My Studio. Completed song files can be backed up privately to Cloudflare R2 for your account.

Music for Hindi, Hinglish and more

Create song concepts across the languages you write in, including Hindi, English, Hinglish, Haryanvi and Punjabi. Voice and language quality depend on the connected model and the instructions you provide. Try being specific about the mood, arrangement and tempo—such as “gentle Hindi acoustic duet with piano and a calm chorus.”

Good prompts make your creative intention easier to communicate, but they cannot guarantee a particular singer, accent, length or musical quality. Do not ask the system to impersonate a real artist or reuse copyrighted lyrics without permission.

Free and premium music options

Eligible free accounts can generate up to 3 AI songs per calendar month, subject to service availability and provider limits. Free audio downloads include MP3 128 and 192 kbps options. Higher-quality MP3, WAV and source-file download controls are reserved for server-verified Premium or Pro accounts. Paid membership checkout is not currently active.

Commercial rights are not automatically granted by a file format or a paid plan label. Review the terms of your source material, the music provider and RhymeOrbit before commercially releasing any generated recording. WAV conversion cannot restore audio detail missing from the source file.

Frequently asked questions

Does this generate vocals or only instrumental music?

The connected music provider is configured for songs with vocals and instruments. Actual results depend on provider availability and generation settings.

How many free songs can I make?

The free plan offers up to three songs per calendar month for eligible accounts. Generation uses the remaining monthly balance shown in AI Studio.

Where are songs saved?

Generated tracks appear in My Studio. Completed songs can be saved to your private cloud library when archiving succeeds. Keep local backups of important work.

Can I sell or release my generated song?

Do not assume commercial permission. Check the provider’s license and any rights in your lyrics, samples and references before publishing commercially.
