LAME / lamejs MP3 encoder

MP3 download quality conversion uses @breezystack/lamejs 1.2.7, a JavaScript port of LAME. The unmodified browser build is served separately at /vendor/lamejs.iife.js and only loaded when MP3 conversion is requested.
