From a rough idea to a complete song

Songwriting is personal. RhymeOrbit's AI Studio can help explore drafts and different creative directions while you decide what stays. Use it as a writing partner for a blank page, an unfinished chorus or a verse that needs a stronger hook.

01 / IDEA

Pick a story

Try a first-love memory, a long-distance friendship, family gratitude, a confident rap or an upbeat celebration.

02 / STYLE

Choose the sound

Describe pop, folk, lo-fi, hip-hop, romantic ballad or any mix of styles. Add a mood, language and tempo idea.

03 / REFINE

Make every line count

Edit rhymes, syllables, the main hook and how the verses connect. Song Doctor offers another perspective on your draft.

Hindi, Hinglish and regional songwriting

Want a Haryanvi folk concept, Hindi romantic lyrics or a Hinglish rap hook? Describe the dialect, audience and emotional tone clearly. You can request different languages where the connected text model supports them. Always review outputs for natural phrasing and the correct local expressions.

For a useful first draft, include the topic, singer perspective, tempo or energy, intended language and a short description of your chorus. You can also paste an unfinished verse and ask for options rather than a completely new song.

Make it your own

AI-generated writing can repeat familiar patterns or make mistakes. Change generic phrases, check that the words match your voice, and avoid copying a known song's distinctive lyrics. Save important drafts to My Studio and export your work before clearing browser data. Text generation is available to eligible signed-in users subject to provider capacity and usage controls.

AI lyrics and Shayari are offered without a fixed monthly song count, but the text provider may still enforce rate limits, availability limits or abuse controls. Creating lyrics does not automatically create an audio recording.

Frequently asked questions

Can I generate Hindi song lyrics?

You can ask for Hindi and other languages supported by the connected AI model. Review the grammar, meaning and regional expressions before using the text.

Can I edit the lyrics after generating?

Yes. AI Studio lets you revise your draft, adjust its structure and save creative projects.

Does the lyrics generator include music?

This page covers text-based songwriting. To generate a recording with vocals and instruments, use the separate AI Song Generator in Studio.

Are AI lyrics free?

Eligible users can use AI writing tools subject to model and service constraints. Vocal song generation has a separate three-song monthly free limit.

Write something worth singing.

Bring your idea into AI Studio, draft original lyrics, and develop them into your next song.
